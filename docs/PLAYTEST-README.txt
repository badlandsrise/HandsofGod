HANDS OF GOD - PLAYTEST BUILD
Badlandsrise Game Studio

Thank you for playing! This is an early build. Eras 1 to 3 (Tribe, Village,
Town) are playable, about 3 hours in all. The playtest ends when Era 3 does;
Era 4 (Launch) is still being built.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO START
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (do not run it from inside the zip).
2. Double-click HandsOfGod.exe.
   If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, click "More info", then
   "Run anyway". The game is not code-signed yet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE GAME IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are a god's hand: a big white cartoon glove. You have just found people on
your world. You cannot do things for them; you can only pick them up and set
them to work. Guide them from a frightened tribe to a stone town, and watch
them come to love you.

The panel at the top left always shows what the people are dreaming of next,
as a checklist. Follow it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Left click a person ......... pick them up
Left click a job or plot .... set them down to work there
Right click ................. set them down with no job
Shift + left click .......... pick up one more person
Left hold and drag .......... scoop everyone in a circle (after Grip II)
Hold E over people/fields ... use a power gesture (Warmth, Blessing, Rain...)
C ........................... call every idle person to the hand
WASD / arrows / screen edge . move the camera
Middle mouse drag ........... move the camera
Q / R ....................... rotate the camera
Mouse wheel ................. zoom
Space ....................... pause
1 / 2 / 3 ................... game speed
Tab ......................... show all labels
F1 .......................... the Village Book (people, family tree, dreams)
F5 / F9 ..................... quick save / quick load
Esc ......................... menu (save, load, options)

Hover over anything (people, buildings, powers, stores) to see what it is and
what it needs.

WHAT WE WOULD LOVE TO HEAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Write notes as you play, like a diary. Anything is useful, and short is fine:
  - Moments where you did not know what to do next, and the in-game day.
  - Anything confusing, tedious, or too fast or too slow.
  - Anything that looked wrong or broke (a screenshot helps: Win+Shift+S).
  - Moments you enjoyed.
  - The day and era you stopped at, and why.

Send your notes back to the person who gave you the game.

If the game crashes or gets stuck, please also send your save files from:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Hands of God\saves
(paste that line into the File Explorer address bar)
